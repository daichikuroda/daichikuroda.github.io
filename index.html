I study the mathematical foundations of hierarchical clustering and community detection. My work asks which properties a clustering method should satisfy and which methods satisfy them.

Advised by Patrick Thiran and Matthias Grossglauser (co-advisor).

  3. JASA 2026

    When Does Bottom-Up Beat Top-Down in Hierarchical Community Detection?

    Maximilien Dreveton, Daichi Kuroda, Matthias Grossglauser, Patrick Thiran

    Journal of the American Statistical Association, 121(554), 1284–1295

    Establishes recovery guarantees for bottom-up hierarchical community detection, including optimal exact recovery thresholds at intermediate levels of the hierarchy.

    Kentaro Morikawa, Daichi Kuroda, Yasuhiro Inoue
